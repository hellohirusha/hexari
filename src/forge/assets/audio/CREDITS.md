# Forge audio credits

These audio files are distributed under CC0 1.0. Credits are included to make
the source of each sound clear.

- Files prefixed `casino-audio_`: Kenney, [Casino Audio](https://kenney.nl/assets/casino-audio), including the dice and card recordings.
- Files prefixed `impact-sounds_`: Kenney, [Impact Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds), including wood, mining, metal and bell recordings.
- Files prefixed `rpg-audio_`: Kenney, [RPG Audio](https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio), including wood chopping and coins.
- `interface-sounds_error_003.ogg`: Kenney, [Interface Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds).
- `sheep.ogg`: AntumDeluge, based on a recording by mikewest, [Sheep Baa](https://opengameart.org/content/sheep-baa).
- `leaves.ogg`: Augmentality (Brandon Morris), [Random Sounds Samples](https://opengameart.org/content/random-sounds-samples), used under the CC0 option.
- `victory.ogg`: ARoachIFoundOnMyPillow, [Victory Fanfare](https://opengameart.org/content/victory-fanfare).
- `defeat.ogg`: Robin Lamb, [Lose Game Short Music Clip](https://opengameart.org/content/lose-game-short-music-clip).
- `crowd-cheer.ogg` and `game-over.ogg`: SpritePilot, [Free Game Over and Victory Sound Effects](https://spritepilot.com/free-game-assets/sound-effects/moments).
- `welcome-music.ogg`: KiluaBoy, [Sunset Walk](https://opengameart.org/content/sunset-walk-ambient-quiet-sweet-loop).
- `crowded-pub.ogg`: Bobjt, [Crowded Pub](https://opengameart.org/content/crowded-pub), 2025 loop.

Kenney recordings were obtained from the public [Open Game SFX Index](https://github.com/Mcamento8/open-game-sfx-index), with SHA-256 checks against its catalog. The other recordings were downloaded from their OpenGameArt pages.
